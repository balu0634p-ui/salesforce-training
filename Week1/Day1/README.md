1. What is CRM?
CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management. It is a system used by companies to manage customer information, sales, and communication. CRM helps businesses improve customer relationships and provide better service.
2. Why Companies Use Salesforce
Companies use Salesforce to:
Store customer data,Track sales and leads,Improve customer support,Automate business processes,Increase business productivity,salesforce helps companies manage everything in one platform.
3. Account:
Account represents a company or organization in Salesforce.
Example:
TCS, Infosys, Amazon
4. Contact:
Contact represents a person related to an account or company.
Example:
Employee or customer details like name, phone number, and email.
5. Opportunity:
Opportunity represents a potential sales deal or business opportunity.
Example:
A company planning to purchase software from another company.
6. Real-World Mapping
In real life, a company is represented as an Account in Salesforce. The people working in or connected to that company are stored as Contacts. When the company shows interest in buying a product or service, it is recorded as an Opportunity.
Example:
Amazon = Account
bala kiran (Manager) = Contact
Software Purchase Deal = Opportunity
